## 2. Market pattern (what nobody does well)

- Nobody shows a working demo. Every site describes a CRM; none lets you drag a deal card.
- Pricing is either hidden (Camir, XCORE) or so low it scares serious buyers (50 EUR landing). Mid-market with transparent "de la" pricing in MDL is empty.
- RO-only or RU-only is the norm. Trilingual with native copy (not translated) is rare; XCORE and Artyonix are the only trilingual ones and their RU reads translated.
- Messenger buttons are missing on most agency sites, even though Moldovan buyers write on WhatsApp/Telegram/Viber before they call.
- No one sells AI SEO / GEO. Artyonix and RDG sell chatbots; nobody sells "apari în răspunsurile AI".
- Stock photos of handshakes and laptops everywhere. Dark, product-led visuals are absent locally.
- No response-time promise anywhere. "Răspundem în < 1 oră" is a differentiator by itself.
- Nobody says who owns the code, what happens after launch, or whether they invoice an SRL with factură fiscală.

## 4. Trust signals that matter here

1. Physical address in Chișinău and a named human with a direct phone.
2. Prices in MDL (lei), with "de la" and what's included; foreign-currency-only quotes read as outsourcing.
3. Factură fiscală for SRL, contract, TVA handled.
4. WhatsApp + Telegram + Viber (Viber is still big with 35+ owners; Telegram with 25-40).
5. Romanian + Russian + English, written natively. Russian-speaking owners are roughly half the buyers in Chișinău, Bălți, Comrat, Tiraspol side.
6. Speed of reply. Local sellers are slow; "răspundem în < 1 oră" beats any badge.
7. Integrations named explicitly: 1C, Contabilitate (1C Contabilitate 8), MPay/PayNet/maib, Facebook Lead Ads, Gmail, WhatsApp Business.
8. "Preț fix în 48 h" removes the biggest fear: the open-ended estimate.
